# Design simplification

The user approved the five improvements from the design audit. Keep the dark palette, condensed display type and pink accent. Make category, charging rule, examples and source the main research flow.

- Home keeps the directory URL and adds a compact category selector and primary comparison action. Remove the rotating capture ticker and large repeated wordmark.
- Use one category selector on comparison and weekly changes pages. Store category and comparison question in the URL. Carry category through navigation and page records.
- Show one selected comparison at a time: charging structure, a commercial policy, or published prices. Every policy answer links to its matching directory population.
- Keep mobile navigation available through an accessible menu. Move Your pages into the account menu and retain its footer link.
- Show terms first on page records. Keep the capture date and archived source visible; place model, rubric, browser and confidence values in disclosures.
- Keep layered page titles to represent saved pricing-page versions. Use literal labels, more readable secondary text and less vertical space. Retain counts, uncertainty and source limits.

Keep publication thresholds. Verify desktop, phone, keyboard navigation, category persistence, answer links and profile disclosures.
